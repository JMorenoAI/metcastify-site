Metcastify Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Metcastify ("the app", "we") is a weather app published by Jing Moreno. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: Metcastify has no accounts and no server of its own that stores your data. Your location is used to fetch weather for where you are, your preferences stay on your device, and advertising is provided by Google AdMob under the consent choices you make.

Information we handle, and why

Weather notifications (rain, lightning, UV, and alert heads-ups) are scheduled locally on your device; no notification data is sent to us.

Location

Metcastify requests location access to show conditions where you are. Coordinates are sent to the weather services below to retrieve forecasts and are not used to identify you, sold, or stored by us. You can decline location access and search for places by name instead; the app then estimates a starting location from your IP address. You can change location permissions at any time in your device settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Metcastify shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the iOS advertising identifier or Android advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, and information about your interactions with ads, as described in Google's advertising policy and Google's privacy policy.

  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: before ads are shown you will see a consent prompt. If you decline personalized ads, ads are shown without personalization. You can change your choice from the app's settings at any time.
  • iOS: personalized ads are only enabled if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization under Settings > Google > Ads.

Optional AI assistant

The Ask feature answers questions using an on-device rules engine by default; nothing leaves your phone. You may optionally switch to the Puter AI engine in Settings. If you do, you sign in to a Puter account, and your questions along with a compact summary of the current forecast are sent to Puter to generate answers. AI usage is billed to your Puter account, not to Metcastify. We never send your questions anywhere unless you have opted in.

Weather data providers

To display weather, the app requests data from these services. Requests include the coordinates being viewed and standard connection information (such as IP address), governed by each provider's own policy:

  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) — headline forecasts on iOS
  • Open-Meteo — forecasts, air quality, and geocoding
  • National Weather Service (weather.gov) — United States alerts and forecasts
  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — aurora and space-weather data
  • RainViewer and LibreWXR — radar imagery
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap — map tiles
  • Where The ISS At — International Space Station position (Explore section)

What we don't do

  • No accounts — the app works without registration, and we cannot identify you.
  • No Metcastify servers store your personal data.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app saves (preferences, saved places, notification settings) lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Data handled by third parties (Google AdMob, Puter, weather providers) is retained under their own policies; the links above describe your options with each.

Children

Metcastify is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because Metcastify stores no personal data on its own servers, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device (permissions, ad settings, deleting the app) or with the third-party services above. For anything else, contact us and we will help.

Security

All network requests the app makes use HTTPS encryption in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy — for example when adding new features or providers — we will update this page and the effective date above.
