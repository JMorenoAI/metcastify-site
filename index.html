Every other weather app assumes you'll do the interpreting. Metcastify does it for you.

The forecast underneath is the same official data everyone else uses. What's different is that you can just ask it a question — in your own words — and get a straight, specific answer.

Built around one idea

Six ways Metcastify turns raw weather data into something you'd actually want to open.

Ask Metcastify

"Good stargazing tonight?" "Best time for a walk?" Type any question in plain English and get a specific, local answer — not a spreadsheet.

Live, layered radar

Track precipitation with radar drawn from four independent sources, plus a minute-by-minute outlook for the next two hours, right where you stand.

Conditions at a glance

UV, wind, humidity, and air quality shown as simple gauges — read in a second, not something you have to look up what "56%" means.

An Explore hub

Outdoor Planner finds your best window outside. Photography tracks golden hour. Gardening warns you before frost. Astronomy tracks the moon. All in one tab.

A sky that shifts with the day

The interface itself moves through sunrise, midday, and sunset color — so a glance at your homescreen tells you the time of day as much as the weather.

Sourced, not synthesized

Every number traces back to the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, or MET Norway. The AI only explains what's already there — it never invents a forecast.

The Explore tab

Every reason you check the weather, in one place.

Not just "will it rain." Metcastify groups the questions people actually have — when to go outside, whether it's safe for the garden, what the sky will look like tonight — into one scrollable hub.

  • Plan — Outdoor Planner, Photography's golden hour, and Travel Mode for comparing saved places.
  • Conditions — Health flags high UV before you head out; Gardening tracks frost risk.
  • Discover — Moon phase, 10-day climate range, and a running log of weather milestones.
