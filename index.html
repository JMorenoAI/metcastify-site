Six ways it's different

Same official data everyone else uses. What's different is you can ask it a question — in your own words — and get a straight answer.

Ask Metcastify

"Good stargazing tonight?" "Best time for a walk?" Type any question and get a specific, local answer — not a spreadsheet.

Live, layered radar

Precipitation from four independent sources, plus a minute-by-minute outlook for the next two hours.

Conditions at a glance

UV, wind, humidity, air quality as simple gauges — read in a second, not something you decode.

Explore hub

Outdoor Planner, golden hour, frost warnings, moon phase — every reason you check the weather, one tab.

Sky that shifts with the day

The interface moves through sunrise, midday, and sunset — a glance tells you the time as much as the weather.

Sourced, not synthesized

Every number traces back to NWS, Open-Meteo, or MET Norway. The app explains what's there — it never invents a forecast.
