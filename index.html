Metcastify

Weather that explains itself

Most weather apps show you numbers. Metcastify tells you what they mean — when the rain actually starts, whether tonight is a good night to see stars, and why tomorrow feels different from today.

What it does

💬

Explains, not just displays

Plain-language summaries of what the forecast means for your day, powered by an on-device engine that works offline.

🌧️

Live radar

Animated precipitation radar and satellite layers with a scrubbing timeline, right where you are.

⚠️

Severe weather alerts

Official National Weather Service watches and warnings, plus rain, lightning and UV heads-ups as notifications.

📅

Planners

Hour-by-hour and ten-day planning views that highlight the windows that matter — commutes, runs, stargazing.

🌌

Sky & space

Sunrise and moon phases, aurora chances from NOAA space weather, and even where the ISS is right now.

🌗

Day & night, beautifully

An interface that shifts with the sky outside, from bright afternoons to starry nights.
